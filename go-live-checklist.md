# Go-Live Readiness Checklist

**Project:** Digital Product Launch  
**Decision meeting:** Week 11  
**Release target:** Week 12

> A checked item should have evidence or a named approver. Do not treat this illustrative checklist as evidence that a real release has passed.

## Product and business
- [ ] MVP scope and release notes approved.
- [ ] Must-have requirements traced to test evidence.
- [ ] Business owner has reviewed and accepted the release.
- [ ] Known limitations and deferred features documented.

## Quality assurance
- [ ] Functional and regression testing completed.
- [ ] Critical user journeys passed.
- [ ] No unresolved critical/high-severity defects, unless an explicit exception is approved.
- [ ] UAT completed and sign-off recorded.
- [ ] Test evidence and defect disposition stored.

## Technology and security
- [ ] Production environment and access verified.
- [ ] Deployment plan reviewed.
- [ ] Backup and rollback/recovery approach documented and tested where appropriate.
- [ ] Monitoring and alerting configured.
- [ ] Security, privacy and compliance reviews completed by responsible specialists.
- [ ] Third-party dependencies confirmed.

## Operations and customer support
- [ ] Support team briefed and trained.
- [ ] FAQs and customer-facing guidance approved.
- [ ] Incident triage and escalation owners confirmed.
- [ ] Support channels and operating hours communicated.
- [ ] Hypercare rota and handover agreed.

## Marketing and communications
- [ ] Launch messaging approved.
- [ ] Website/app store or release communications ready, where applicable.
- [ ] Internal stakeholders informed.
- [ ] Customer communication timing agreed.
