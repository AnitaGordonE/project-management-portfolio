## 3. Critical dependencies
- Requirements must be prioritised before design and development are baselined.
- Approved designs and technical setup are prerequisites for full development.
- A stable build is required before system testing and UAT.
- UAT and operational readiness are prerequisites for go/no-go approval.
- Deployment is conditional on approval and release controls.

## 4. Schedule management
Review the schedule weekly. Track baseline dates separately from forecast and actual dates. Record variance, cause, impact, corrective action and owner. Do not silently move baseline dates; use change control when the approved delivery commitment changes.
