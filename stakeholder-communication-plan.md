# Stakeholder & Communication Plan

## 1. Stakeholder register
| Stakeholder | Interest / influence | Engagement approach | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor | High / High | Decision-focused summary, milestone and exception escalation | Project Manager |
| Product Manager | High / High | Backlog reviews, scope and acceptance decisions | Project Manager |
| Engineering Lead | High / High | Delivery planning, dependency and blocker reviews | Project Manager |
| Design Lead | High / Medium | Design reviews and hand-off checkpoints | Product Manager |
| QA Lead | High / Medium | Test planning, defect triage and exit recommendation | Project Manager |
| Operations Lead | High / Medium | Readiness, support process and handover | Project Manager |
| Marketing Lead | Medium / Medium | Launch calendar, content and approval checkpoints | Project Manager |
| End-user representatives | High / Low individually | Research, usability feedback and UAT | Product Manager |

## 2. Communication matrix
| Communication | Audience | Frequency | Channel / format | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily team update | Delivery team | Daily, async | Task board / team channel | Workstream Leads |
| Delivery stand-up | Core team | 2–3 times weekly | Short video call or async update | Project Manager |
| Project status report | Sponsor and leads | Weekly | Written report | Project Manager |
| Backlog refinement | Product and delivery | Weekly | Backlog session | Product Manager |
| Risk and issue review | Core leads | Weekly | RAID review | Project Manager |
| Steering / milestone review | Sponsor and leads | At key milestones | Decision meeting | Project Manager |
| UAT review | Business users and QA | During Week 10 | Test session and sign-off | QA Lead |
| Go/no-go meeting | Sponsor and accountable leads | Week 11 | Readiness review | Project Manager |

## 3. Meeting and async working practices
- Share agendas and pre-reads in advance.
- Record decisions, actions, owners and due dates.
- Use a shared source of truth for schedule, scope and RAID.
- Provide concise written updates for colleagues who cannot attend.
- Escalate blockers with context, impact and a proposed next step.

## 4. Escalation path
1. Workstream owner attempts resolution and records the blocker.
2. Project Manager assesses cross-project impact and coordinates support.
3. Escalate to the sponsor or steering group when a decision, material trade-off or baseline change is required.
4. Record the decision, owner and follow-up date in the decision log.
