# Project Risk Register

**Scoring:** Probability (P) and Impact (I) are rated 1–5. Score = P × I.  
**Suggested bands:** 1–4 Low; 5–9 Medium; 10–15 High; 16–25 Critical.

> Scores below are initial illustrative assessments, not measured project data.

| ID | Risk | P | I | Score | Response / mitigation | Owner | Trigger |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| R01 | Requirements or approvals are delayed | 4 | 4 | 16 | Set review deadlines, decision log and escalation path | Project Manager | Approval overdue |
| R02 | Scope expands after baseline | 4 | 4 | 16 | Prioritise MVP; assess every change for impact; obtain approval | Product Manager | New request affects baseline |
| R03 | Development takes longer than estimated | 3 | 5 | 15 | Validate estimates, track sprint capacity and surface blockers early | Engineering Lead | Sprint goal at risk |
| R04 | Integration or environment issues delay testing | 3 | 4 | 12 | Test integrations early; provision environments in advance | Engineering Lead | Failed integration test |
| R05 | Critical defects remain near release | 3 | 5 | 15 | Early QA, severity-based triage, regression testing and release gate | QA Lead | Critical/high defect open |
| R06 | UAT participants are unavailable | 3 | 4 | 12 | Book UAT windows early and nominate backups | Business Owner | UAT attendance not confirmed |
| R07 | Support team is not ready for launch | 3 | 4 | 12 | Prepare FAQs, training, escalation and ownership before go-live | Operations Lead | Readiness checklist incomplete |
| R08 | Key team member becomes unavailable | 3 | 3 | 9 | Cross-train, document work and identify backup owners | Workstream Leads | Capacity gap identified |
| R09 | Security or privacy issue is identified late | 2 | 5 | 10 | Involve qualified reviewers early and track remediation to closure | Security / Engineering | Review finding raised |
| R10 | Launch communications are delayed or inaccurate | 2 | 3 | 6 | Use an approved communications plan and content sign-off | Marketing Lead | Content not approved |

## Risk response rules
- **Critical:** Escalate immediately to the sponsor; agree an action plan and review frequently.
- **High:** Assign a named action owner and review at least weekly.
- **Medium:** Monitor at project review and act if exposure increases.
- **Low:** Maintain visibility and reassess when circumstances change.

## Issue management
A risk is an uncertain future event; an issue is something that has already happened. Log issues separately with impact, owner, due date, action and escalation status.

## Review cadence
Review the register at the weekly project meeting and whenever a major change, dependency failure or release decision occurs.
